Verify a manifest: (image_hash32, signature) -> vendor key.

The caller has already confirmed that the staged binary's SHA-256 equals image_hash. We do not re-hash here — that's the bootloader's job (we may not even have enough RAM to hold the full image).

Returns
true iff the SE050 reports the signature as valid under slot::kOtaVendorPublicKey.
