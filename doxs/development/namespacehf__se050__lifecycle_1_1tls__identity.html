Demo: sign a synthetic "TLS CertificateVerify transcript hash" using the SE050 identity key.

In a real TLS session mbedTLS would compute a SHA-256 transcript hash of the handshake so far and hand it to our hook. We simulate that step so you can see the latency of an SE050-backed handshake on your bench.

Returns
true on success, with signature bytes dumped to the log.
